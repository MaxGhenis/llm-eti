# Data, code, and declarations {.unnumbered}

**Data and code availability.** Source code, the frozen scenario sample,
archived responses, checksums, and build instructions are available in the
[project repository](https://github.com/MaxGhenis/llm-eti). Redistribution
remains subject to the third-party terms described in
[`NOTICE.md`](https://github.com/MaxGhenis/llm-eti/blob/main/NOTICE.md).

**Human-subjects statement.** No human participants were recruited, contacted,
observed, or assigned an intervention. The study analyzes archived
language-model outputs and non-identifying PolicyEngine-derived tax-unit
scenarios; it does not analyze human responses or identifiable private
information. Human-subjects review and participant consent were therefore not
applicable.

**Use of AI.** Language-model outputs are the object of study. OpenAI Codex also
assisted with repository refactoring, analysis verification, tests, and
manuscript drafting. The authors remain responsible for the methods,
interpretation, and final text.
