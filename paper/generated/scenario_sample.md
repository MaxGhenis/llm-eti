The legacy script first drew 550 tax units with replacement from each year, concatenated 2023 before 2024, removed perturbed rates outside the observed initial-rate range, and retained the first 1,000 rows. The frozen result consequently contains **550 scenarios from 2023** and **450 from 2024**, rather than equal year counts. This order-dependent construction and the positive-income source eligibility restriction define the scope of the archive.
