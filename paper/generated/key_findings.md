The four models do not produce a common response distribution. Claude Haiku 4.5 has a median scenario-level implied response ratio of **0.81**, and DeepSeek V3 has a median of **0.37**. Gemma 4 and GPT-4o mini both have medians of approximately zero because most responses leave taxable income unchanged (87.3% and 69.7%). Their occasional large adjustments nevertheless produce positive least-squares slopes. This mean–median divergence reflects a large mass at the supplied baseline and sensitivity to tail responses; it is not evidence that any model recovers a human behavioral elasticity.
